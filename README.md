# ♻️ ReCircle

### Give Your Unused Things a Second Life.

**ReCircle** is a hyperlocal circular-economy platform that makes
sustainable choices easier, more rewarding, and more accessible. It
connects people with nearby opportunities to **sell, donate, swap,
repair, reuse, or recycle** unused products while tracking the
environmental impact of every action.

> **Our goal:** Make the sustainable choice the easiest and most
> rewarding choice.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 🌱 The Problem

People often want to make environmentally responsible choices, but
sustainable actions can feel inconvenient or difficult to track.

At the same time, valuable products such as electronics, furniture,
clothes, books, and household items are frequently discarded because
there is no frictionless way to:

-   Find someone nearby who needs an unused item
-   Decide whether an item should be repaired or replaced
-   Find appropriate repair and recycling facilities
-   Understand the environmental impact of everyday choices
-   Stay motivated to build sustainable habits

ReCircle brings these activities together in one connected circular
ecosystem.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 💡 Our Solution

ReCircle combines a **hyperlocal marketplace, AI-powered
recommendations, sustainability impact tracking, community challenges,
and rewards**.

Instead of treating an unwanted product as waste, ReCircle asks:

**Can this item have another life?**

The platform guides users through the circular journey:

**BUY → USE → REPAIR → REUSE → SELL / SWAP → RECYCLE**

## ⭐ Key Features

### 🛒 Hyperlocal Circular Marketplace

-   Sell, donate, swap, or repair unused products
-   Add product details, photos, and location
-   Nearby listings and map-based discovery
-   Filters for easier searching
-   Buyer Smart Value Score
-   Seller Selling Opportunity Score
-   Demand-driven listings
-   Wishlist and reverse-search integration

### 🤖 AI Intelligence

-   Smart product recommendations
-   Price estimation
-   Demand prediction
-   Repair vs. replace analysis
-   Personalized sustainability suggestions
-   Listing-quality suggestions
-   Currently the image inserted should be less than 100 kb

### 🔎 Reverse Search / Wishlist

Users can post what they **need**, instead of only browsing what is
available.

The platform can use these demand signals to notify nearby users who may
already own the requested item.

**Demand Signal → Match → Listing**

### 🎁 Real-World Rewards

EcoPoints can be connected to tangible incentives such as:

-   Campus credits
-   Discounts
-   Community rewards
-   Recognition and achievements

### 🌿 Green Squads

Users can participate in sustainability communities based on:

-   Colleges
-   Societies
-   Neighborhoods
-   Cities

Communities can organize shared goals, challenges, reuse drives, repair
drives, and donation campaigns.

### 📊 Sustainability Impact Tracking

ReCircle visualizes the estimated impact of user actions, including:

-   CO₂ saved
-   Waste diverted
-   Resources saved

All impact figures are presented as **estimates**, where applicable.

### 🏆 Gamification & EcoPoints

Sustainable actions can earn EcoPoints and unlock:

-   Daily / weekly / monthly challenges
-   Streaks
-   Achievement badges
-   Individual leaderboards
-   Community leaderboards
-   EcoPoints wallet

Example achievements include:

-   🌱 Green Starter
-   ♻️ Recycling Hero
-   🔧 Repair Master
-   🔄 Circular Champion
-   🌍 Eco Leader

### 🛠️ Repair & Recycling Network

Help users discover nearby:

-   Repair shops
-   Recycling centers
-   NGOs and donation centers
-   E-waste collection points

The network can include map-based discovery, distance information,
ratings, and services.

## 🚀 What Makes ReCircle Different?

### 1. Circular Economy, Not Just Recycling

ReCircle prioritizes keeping products useful for longer rather than
treating recycling as the only sustainable solution.

### 2. Supply + Demand Connection

Most marketplaces focus on what people are selling. ReCircle also
captures **what people need**, allowing demand to help create useful
matches.

### 3. Repair Before Replace

AI-assisted repair-vs-replace analysis encourages users to consider
extending a product's life.

### 4. Hyperlocal by Design

Nearby users, repair services, NGOs, and recycling facilities can reduce
friction and make sustainable actions more practical.

### 5. Sustainability + Motivation

Environmental impact tracking is combined with EcoPoints, challenges,
badges, and community participation.
